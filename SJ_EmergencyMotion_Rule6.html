II. LEGAL STANDARD

A Single Justice may grant interlocutory relief under Mass. R. App. P. 6 upon consideration of four factors: (1) the likelihood of success on the merits; (2) whether denial of the stay will cause irreparable harm; (3) whether the stay will substantially harm other parties; and (4) where the public interest lies. Packaging Indus. Group, Inc. v. Cheney, 380 Mass. 609, 617 (1980). The moving party need not demonstrate a probability of success; a serious question is sufficient where the other factors strongly favor relief. GTE Prods. Corp. v. Stewart, 414 Mass. 721, 723 (1993).
